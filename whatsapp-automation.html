Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to use WhatsApp automation on our official business phone number? +

Yes. We build strictly on the official Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API. Unlike unofficial browser-scraping tools, the official API keeps your business number compliant and protected.

Can the WhatsApp chatbot understand Bangla and Banglish messages? +

Yes. Our AI assistants understand standard English, বাংলা (Bangla script), and conversational Banglish (Bangla written in English letters) and reply naturally in the customer's preferred language.

Can a human team member step in and take over a chat at any time? +

Absolutely. If a customer asks a complex question or your team replies manually, the AI pauses automation for that conversation and notifies your staff.

How do Meta WhatsApp API conversation charges work? +

Appiex charges a transparent development and setup fee for building your system. Meta's per-conversation messaging fees (for marketing or utility templates) and AI model token costs are billed directly at official platform rates with zero hidden markup.
