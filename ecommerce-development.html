BUYER QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does ecommerce website development cost in Bangladesh? +

Ecommerce website cost depends on catalog complexity, payment gateways (bKash, Nagad, SSLCOMMERZ), courier automation, and whether you need custom Next.js engineering or a standard CMS. Request a scoped quote or see our Bangladesh Website Cost Guide for a full breakdown.

Can you integrate bKash, Nagad, SSLCOMMERZ, and Cash on Delivery? +

Yes. We integrate official bKash tokenized checkout, Nagad merchant APIs, SSLCOMMERZ (including multi-bank 0% EMI), Stripe for international cards, and Cash on Delivery with OTP/phone verification.

Do you connect Pathao, Steadfast, and RedX courier APIs? +

Yes. Orders placed on your website can be pushed to Pathao, Steadfast, or RedX with a single click from your admin dashboard, automatically updating order status as parcels are delivered.
