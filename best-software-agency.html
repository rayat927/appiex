How to Choose the Best Software Agency For You

Before hiring an agency in Bangladesh or internationally, compare the following criteria to reduce delivery risk:

01. Tech Stack Modernity

Select technology based on product needs, maintenance, hosting and your team's skills. Ask why each proposed framework fits, and what trade-offs or migration costs it creates.

02. Speed & Milestone Velocity

Request project-specific milestones, acceptance criteria, staging demonstrations and realistic review windows. Delivery speed depends on integrations, content and testing.

03. Transparent Rates ($30–$80 vs $150)

Request itemized quotes covering discovery, delivery, testing, revisions and post-launch support. Compare total scope and ownership instead of unverified hourly benchmarks.

04. 100% NDA & IP Ownership

Confirm confidentiality, third-party dependencies, source-code access, license obligations and intellectual-property transfer in the written agreement.
