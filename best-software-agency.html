How to Choose the Best Software Agency For You

Before hiring any agency in Bangladesh or internationally, evaluate these four critical criteria to guarantee project success:

01. Tech Stack Modernity

Avoid agencies pushing legacy PHP or monolithic setups for modern web apps. Ensure your agency builds on Next.js 14/15, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and edge serverless infrastructures that scale without technical debt.

02. Speed & Milestone Velocity

In modern tech, shipping in 2 to 4 weeks beats a 6-month waterfall build. Choose an agency that works in agile 2-week sprint demos with live staging URLs.

03. Transparent Rates ($30–$80 vs $150)

Enterprise software houses carry massive real estate and management overhead that inflates rates to $100–$150/hr. Boutique engineering agencies like Appiex offer identical or superior senior engineers at $30–$80/hr.

04. 100% NDA & IP Ownership

Always demand a written bilateral NDA before sharing requirements, and verify that 100% of the git repository code and intellectual property belongs to you upon milestone payment.
