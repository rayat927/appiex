Editorial & Corrections Policy

This policy explains how Appiex prepares technical and commercial guidance. Our goal is useful, accurate content—not manufactured authority or guaranteed outcomes.

Authorship and review

Each indexable article identifies its responsible author or reviewer. Author pages describe the subject areas for which that person is responsible. We do not create fictional experts or use third-party identities.

Sources and original work

Important external claims should link to a primary source such as official documentation, standards or first-party product information. Original tests, audits and case studies must document the method, sample, date and limitations. A marketing example is labelled as an example.

Pricing and performance

Published prices and timelines are planning ranges unless a signed proposal says otherwise. Performance, ranking, conversion and revenue outcomes depend on scope, baseline, market and implementation. Appiex does not guarantee a Google position or a universal business result.

Client evidence and reviews

Client names, logos, quotations and numerical results are published only with permission and supporting evidence. Technology logos describe tools in use; they do not imply a partnership unless that relationship is confirmed.

Updates and corrections

Material changes update the page's modified date. If you find an error, email contact@appiex.com with the page URL and supporting information. We review correction requests and update or retract claims that cannot be substantiated.

AI assistance

Automation may assist research organization, drafting or editing. A responsible human must review indexable articles for factual accuracy, usefulness, sourcing and originality before publication.
