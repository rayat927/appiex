Our process

Business & Market Audit

We start by reviewing your current business goals, website performance, sales numbers, and competitors to see what is working and what is holding your growth back.

Customer & Offer Research

We identify your best-paying customers, what problems they want solved, and how to position your products or services so the value is immediately clear.

Funnel & Channel Architecture

We map out your complete digital ecosystem—showing how website pages, SEO, social media, ads, and automation will work together to bring steady leads and sales.

Actionable Growth Roadmap

We turn the strategy into a clear, step-by-step action plan with priorities, timelines, and budget targets so your team knows exactly what to execute first.

Tracking, Review & Scaling

Once the strategy goes live, we track real sales and conversion data, refine the funnel, and help you scale the channels that bring the highest return.

FAQ

What is included in a Digital Strategy project with Appiex?

We provide market and competitor research, brand positioning, target customer profiling, sales funnel planning, website and product roadmaps, channel strategy (SEO, social, ads, automation), and clear KPI tracking.

How do I know if my business needs a digital strategy first?

If you are getting website visitors or running ads without steady sales, launching a new brand or product, or unsure where to spend your budget for the best return, a clear digital strategy saves you from costly trial and error.

Do you only give advice, or can you also build and launch everything?

We do both. Once your strategy and roadmap are approved, our in-house team can design the UI/UX, develop the website or app, set up AI automation, and run your SEO and marketing campaigns.

Can you work alongside our internal marketing or sales team?

Yes. We often create the growth roadmap and funnel structure, then guide your internal marketing, content, and sales team step by step so everyone executes with clear goals.

How do you measure if the digital strategy is working?

We focus on simple, real business numbers: website conversion rate, cost per lead, customer acquisition cost, repeat orders, and total monthly revenue growth.

Can you help us launch a brand-new product or e-commerce store?

Yes. We build complete Go-To-Market launch plans—covering product pricing, bundle offers, landing page structure, launch campaigns, and customer retention flows.

Will the strategy be easy for our team to understand and follow?

Absolutely. We avoid confusing jargon and long theory documents. You get a clean, visual, step-by-step action plan that shows exactly what to do, in what order, and why.

How long does it take to create a complete digital strategy?

A focused brand and sales funnel audit takes 1–2 weeks. A complete go-to-market plan or full multi-channel growth strategy typically takes 3–6 weeks depending on your business size.

What do we need to prepare before starting a strategy session?

You do not need any formal document. Just share your current website or social pages, tell us what you sell, and let us know your main sales goals—we will handle the research and planning from there.

Ready to plan your next stage of growth?

Tell us where your business is today and what goals you want to hit—we will help you map the clearest path forward.
