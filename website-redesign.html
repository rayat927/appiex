Frequently Asked Questions

How do you redesign a website without losing Google SEO rankings? +

Before writing a single line of code, we export all indexed URLs and top-performing queries from Google Search Console. We preserve high-ranking URL slugs wherever possible, implement permanent 301 redirects for any changed paths, and carry over verified title tags, headings, and JSON-LD schema.

How long does a business or ecommerce website redesign take? +

A standard corporate or service business website redesign takes 2 to 4 weeks. A custom e-commerce redesign with payment gateway, inventory, and courier migration typically takes 4 to 6 weeks.

Can you redesign only specific high-traffic pages instead of the whole site? +

Yes. If your core backend is stable, we can redesign your homepage, core service landing pages, or checkout funnel first to improve conversion rates quickly.

Before redesigning a website that already gets traffic

Ask your website redesign agency to preserve what currently works. Catalogue important landing pages, search queries, enquiries, downloadable assets and internal links before changing templates or URLs. Test the new mobile experience and conversion events with a staging release.

  • SEO migration: map old URLs to relevant new pages, verify redirects and update the sitemap.
  • Conversion: check calls to action, forms, contact buttons and analytics events.
  • Performance: compare Core Web Vitals with reproducible tests and real-user data.
