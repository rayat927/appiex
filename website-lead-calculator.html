A BETTER WAY TO DECIDE

How the numbers work

These formulas show why qualified demand matters more than chasing anonymous visitors. They are simple assumptions, not a conversion forecast.

01

Website enquiries

Monthly website visits × lead conversion rate ÷ 100. A higher click-through rate in Google does not automatically increase enquiries after a visitor arrives.

02

Qualified leads

Enquiries × qualified enquiry percentage ÷ 100. If 100 people enquire but only 10 are real buyers, the qualification rate matters.

03

Expected customers

Qualified leads × close rate ÷ 100. This is an average across a hypothetical set of enquiries; actual customers are whole people.

04

Potential revenue

Expected clients × average client revenue. This excludes costs and capacity constraints, so it is not profit, guaranteed ROI or a business valuation.

THE IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

CTR isn't the same as conversion rate

Search CTR measures search clicks divided by search impressions. Your page title and relevance influence whether people choose your result, but Google can generate its own title and snippet.

Website lead conversion rate measures real enquiries divided by website visits. Clear service fit, proof, page usability and an easy contact path influence this second step.

Qualified lead rate measures whether enquiries become real sales opportunities. A smaller audience with serious projects can be more valuable than a much larger, irrelevant audience.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Questions about website leads and conversions

Does this tool predict my actual sales?

No. It calculates a hypothetical scenario from the numbers you enter. Traffic quality, seasonality, form friction, staffing and customer behavior can change actual results considerably.

What is a good website conversion rate?

There is no reliable universal rate. A SaaS trial, high-ticket agency consultation and ecommerce purchase represent different intents. Compare your own qualified leads and signed clients over comparable periods.

How do I calculate search CTR?

Google Search CTR equals clicks divided by impressions, multiplied by 100. Use Google Search Console and separate branded searches from commercial nonbranded queries where the data allows.

What if my scenario conversion rate is lower?

Negative numbers show a downside scenario. This is useful when considering a redesign or less-qualified traffic source.

Will Appiex collect my numbers?

This calculator runs in your browser. It does not submit the entered figures to a server. The site may still use ordinary page analytics as described in its privacy policy.
