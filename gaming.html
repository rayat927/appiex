05 / BEFORE WE BUILD

Good questions.
Better worlds.

What to expect from custom game development and interactive 3D projects.

Can Appiex develop custom browser games and WebGL experiences?

Yes. We plan and build custom HTML5 browser games, WebGL experiences and interactive 3D websites around the audience, performance budget and project goals.

Can you design a branded game or gamified marketing campaign?

Yes. Depending on the brief, Appiex can create original mini-games, product interactions and branded gamification experiences. Performance and conversion impact should be measured, not assumed.

Do you build interactive 3D product showrooms?

We can design virtual showrooms and browser-based product experiences with interactions, mobile-friendly controls and accessible fallbacks where appropriate.

Which technologies do you use for game and 3D development?

The stack depends on the use case. Options include WebGL, Three.js, JavaScript, Blender and established game engines. We choose tools after checking device support, scope and maintenance needs.

How much does a custom game or 3D website cost?

Costs depend on gameplay mechanics, art production, platform targets, integrations and testing. Share the idea and we can outline a scoped proposal and delivery milestones.
