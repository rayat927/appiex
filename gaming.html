05 // QUICK ANSWERS

Questions before
you press start.

Thoughtful game development and immersive web design start with the right questions. Here are a few we hear often.

Can Appiex build custom browser games and WebGL experiences?+

Yes. We scope browser-based games and interactive 3D experiences around your audience, devices, accessibility needs and performance goals. The right rendering technology depends on the project.

Can interactive 3D experiences help a business grow?+

They can help customers explore products, understand complex ideas and spend more meaningful time with a brand. We recommend testing engagement and conversion impact rather than assuming 3D alone improves sales.

What is the difference between WebGL and game-engine development?+

WebGL-based experiences are often a fit for browser-first interactions and product visualization. Game engines may be useful for more complex worlds, game mechanics or real-time simulations. We choose tools after reviewing scope and platform requirements.

Will a 3D website work on mobile devices?+

Mobile support is planned from the start using responsive layouts, adjustable quality, efficient assets and fallbacks. Performance depends on model complexity, network and device capability.

How much does custom game or 3D experience development cost?+

Pricing depends on content, art assets, mechanics, interactions, platform support and integrations. We start with a discovery conversation and provide a scoped proposal with milestones and deliverables.
