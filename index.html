GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

Before we build.

Answers to practical questions about scope, pricing and delivery.

How long does website or software development take?

Timelines depend on features, integrations, content and review cycles. After discovery we propose milestones covering design, development, testing and launch.

How is a project priced and paid for?

We provide a scope-specific proposal with deliverables, payment stages and acceptance criteria. Read our website development cost guide to compare common budget factors.

Can Appiex build AI agents and integrate business tools?

Yes. We build AI-assisted and rule-based workflows for CRM, messaging, lead handling and internal operations, choosing integrations and human approvals according to the use case. Compare AI agents and n8n workflows.

What happens after launch?

Handover, documentation and any support or maintenance period are specified in the project agreement. Support coverage depends on the agreed scope.

Can agencies partner with Appiex?

Yes. Our white-label partnership options cover scoped engineering delivery, responsibilities, confidentiality and review processes.
