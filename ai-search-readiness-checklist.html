AI Search Readiness Checklist: 14 Checks for SEO, AEO & GEO

A practical checklist for teams that want to improve search discoverability and create answers people and AI-assisted search systems can understand. No signup or expensive software required.

Start the checklistExplore AI search services

What is AI search readiness?

AI search readiness means your valuable pages are accessible, accurately describe your business, answer relevant questions, and provide evidence others can reference. The goal is better eligibility for discovery—not guaranteed AI citations or rankings. Search engines decide which content to surface.

This checklist reflects publicly documented search best practices. It is not an official Google or Microsoft certification, and a high checklist score does not predict a search ranking.

Practical audit

Check these 14 items on your most valuable pages

  1. 01 / AUDIT

    Verify discoverability

    Check: Fetch key pages as an anonymous visitor. Confirm HTTP 200, reachable links and meaningful content in the response HTML.

    Flag: A key landing page renders a blank shell, requires login or returns the wrong status.

  2. 02 / AUDIT

    Confirm index directives

    Check: Inspect robots.txt, page-level robots meta tags and any X-Robots-Tag header. Only index pages worth finding.

    Flag: An important service page has noindex, or a draft is unintentionally indexable.

  3. 03 / AUDIT

    Match canonical URLs

    Check: Pick one preferred URL for every page. Verify canonical markup, www redirects and legacy .html redirects.

    Flag: The page canonical points to a different topic or redirect chain.

  4. 04 / AUDIT

    Audit XML sitemap quality

    Check: List only canonical, indexable URLs and submit the sitemap in Search Console. Keep lastmod dates meaningful.

    Flag: The sitemap contains drafts, deleted pages or duplicate URLs.

  5. 05 / AUDIT

    Test useful, crawlable internal links

    Check: Link from the homepage and service hubs to relevant guides with descriptive visible anchors.

    Flag: An important service page can only be reached through client-side controls.

  6. 06 / AUDIT

    Answer the searcher's question early

    Check: Write a two-to-four sentence direct answer near the top, then add examples, constraints, edge cases and next steps.

    Flag: The page opens with generic marketing copy that never answers the question.

  7. 07 / AUDIT

    Show primary evidence

    Check: Include real methods, screen captures with dates, original demos, examples or transparent limitations.

    Flag: Performance or ranking claims have no traceable proof.

  8. 08 / AUDIT

    Keep the business entity consistent

    Check: Make the brand name, location, service descriptions, ownership and contact method consistent on important pages.

    Flag: Different pages give conflicting team size, awards, addresses or years of experience.

  9. 09 / AUDIT

    Use truthful structured data

    Check: Validate supported Service, Organization, Breadcrumb or Article markup and confirm it matches visible content.

    Flag: Schema contains testimonials, ratings or offers that readers cannot verify.

  10. 10 / AUDIT

    Make pages usable and fast

    Check: Test on mobile and desktop; inspect LCP, INP, CLS, images, contrast and keyboard navigation.

    Flag: Large scripts delay main content or interactive actions are inaccessible.

  11. 11 / AUDIT

    Publish useful comparisons and decision criteria

    Check: Explain alternatives and tradeoffs, including when AI is not needed and when existing tools are sufficient.

    Flag: The content promises every business needs an expensive AI agent.

  12. 12 / AUDIT

    Measure search outcomes

    Check: Segment branded/nonbranded queries; record impressions, clicks, CTR, index coverage and qualified enquiries.

    Flag: Reports celebrate a high branded CTR while commercial service pages receive few clicks.

  13. 13 / AUDIT

    Track AI visibility carefully

    Check: Check observable referral sources and brand citations where available; distinguish estimates from actual analytics.

    Flag: A dashboard claims exact ChatGPT citations without a documented data source.

  14. 14 / AUDIT

    Earn relevant mentions ethically

    Check: Share a truly useful resource with practitioners, answer community questions, and request editorial review—not paid ranking links.

    Flag: Mass directory submissions or forum comments exist only to place a backlink.

How to score your audit: mark each item Pass, Needs work or Not applicable based on evidence. Prioritize access, indexing and clear information before publishing more pages. Avoid treating a total score as a ranking probability.

Where to start

Fix in the right order

  1. Critical: inaccessible pages, noindex errors, bad canonicals, broken redirects and mismatched service information.
  2. High: weak commercial page answers, unclear service scope, unverifiable claims and confusing conversion paths.
  3. Medium: original comparisons, better descriptive internal links, supporting visuals, qualified referrals and publisher outreach.
  4. Ongoing: Search Console trend reviews, analytics testing and user feedback.

Example: diagnosing a page with impressions but no clicks

Suppose a service page receives search impressions but few visitors. Start by checking the query type: branded, informational or commercial. Compare the title and description with the real page content. Then inspect average position, competing results, regional fit and whether the page answers the question immediately. Do not rewrite every title at once; change a small set and compare like-for-like periods.

The clearest improvement is often a more informative title, a specific first-screen explanation and a compelling reason to read further. CTR is not the only measure: a qualified consultation can matter more than dozens of unrelated visits.
