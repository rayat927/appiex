Our process

Workflow & Business Audit

We start by looking at how your business works today. We find which daily tasks take the most time, where leads or messages are getting delayed, and which parts can be automated for the biggest impact.

AI Strategy & Tool Mapping

We design a simple, clear automation plan. Whether you need a Facebook & WhatsApp bot, a research agent, or a full Business Manager, we choose the right AI models and tools that fit your budget and goals.

System Build & AI Training

We build the automation workflows and train the AI using your actual business information, product list, FAQs, and brand voice so it responds accurately and handles tasks reliably.

App Integration & Testing

We connect the AI system with your existing pages, WhatsApp number, website, Google Sheets, or CRM. Everything is tested carefully with real scenarios before going live.

Launch, Training & Support

We launch your automation system, show your team how to use the dashboard in simple steps, and monitor performance to make sure everything runs smoothly 24/7.

FAQ

What can you automate with Facebook and WhatsApp automation?

We can automate auto-replies to Facebook comments and inbox messages, answer customer questions on WhatsApp 24/7, take orders, send payment and delivery updates, collect customer phone numbers, and send broadcast offers automatically.

Will the AI replies sound robotic or natural to my customers?

They sound completely natural and friendly. We train the AI on your own product details, prices, FAQs, and brand tone. It can chat smoothly in English, Bangla, or Banglish just like a real member of your team.

How does your Research Automation system work?

Our AI research agents automatically browse websites, check competitor prices, track market trends, and collect verified leads or articles. Instead of spending hours reading and copy-pasting, you get a clean summary report in minutes.

What is included in the AI Business Manager system?

The AI Business Manager connects your sales, customer messages, orders, billing, and team tasks into one easy dashboard. It automatically assigns leads to your team, tracks daily revenue, sends invoices, and gives you daily business updates.

Can you connect the AI with our existing website, CRM, or Google Sheets?

Yes. We can connect your automation directly with Google Sheets, Shopify, WordPress, WooCommerce, HubSpot, Slack, Notion, or your custom software so all your data stays updated automatically.

Can a human team member step in and take over a chat anytime?

Yes, anytime. If a customer asks a complex question or wants to speak with a person, the AI immediately notifies your team and hands over the chat smoothly.

Is it safe to use on our official Facebook Page and WhatsApp number?

Yes, 100% safe. We use official Meta and WhatsApp Business Cloud APIs along with secure workflow systems so your page and phone number stay protected and compliant.

How long does it take to set up an AI automation system?

A Facebook or WhatsApp AI bot usually takes 1–2 weeks to build and test. A custom Research Automation system takes 2–3 weeks, and a complete AI Business Manager typically takes 4–8 weeks.

Do I need technical knowledge to use the system after launch?

Not at all. We build everything for you, keep the dashboard very simple, and train your team step by step. We also provide ongoing support whenever you want to update offers or add new features.
