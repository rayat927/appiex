PROGRAM LEGAL TERMS

Appendix A: Referral Program Terms & Rules

Framework terms governing official client introductions, commission rates, and payout policies.

Version 1.0 • 2026
•

15% First Project + 5% Repeat Work: Earn 15% of Net Project Value on the client's first signed contract, plus 5% recurring commission on repeat work signed within 12 months.

•

90-Day Lead Protection & Timestamp Priority: Every valid lead submitted via appiex.com/refer is locked to your account for 90 days. First submission wins attribution.

•

Fast 7-Day Cleared Payouts: Paid directly within 7 business days after client payment clears via Wise, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or bKash/Nagad ($25 minimum payout).

Appiex Legal Entity • Dhaka, Bangladesh

1. About these terms

These terms govern the Appiex Referral Program ("Program") run by Appiex ("Appiex", "we"), registered in Dhaka, Bangladesh. By submitting a referral you ("Referrer") agree to these terms.

2. Eligibility

  1. You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter a binding agreement.
  2. Employees, contractors and directors of Appiex, and their immediate family members, can't take part.
  3. You can't refer yourself, a business you own or control, or a business that employs you in a decision-making role about the purchase.
  4. Joining is free. There are no targets or minimum referrals.

3. What counts as a valid referral

  1. It is submitted through the official form at appiex.com/refer. WhatsApp or verbal introductions must be followed by a form submission to be tracked.
  2. The client is a real business or person who has agreed to be contacted by Appiex.
  3. The client is not already an Appiex client and has not been in contact with Appiex about a project in the 6 months before your submission.
  4. The client signs a written agreement (or accepts a proposal) with Appiex within the Lead Protection Period (section 4).

4. Lead protection

  1. Each valid referral is protected for 90 days from the timestamp of your form submission ("Lead Protection Period").
  2. If two Referrers submit the same client, the first valid submission wins.
  3. If the client signs after the Lead Protection Period, no commission is due unless Appiex agrees in writing.

5. Commission

  1. Rate: 15% of the Net Project Value of the client's first signed project ("First Project").
  2. Repeat work: 5% of the Net Project Value of any further project that client signs within 12 months of the First Project's signing date.
  3. Net Project Value: Means the amount the client actually pays Appiex for design and development work. It excludes VAT and other taxes, payment-gateway and bank fees, and third-party costs passed through to the client (domains, hosting, paid licences or plugins, advertising spend, stock content).
  4. Ongoing retainers, maintenance plans and hosting fees are excluded unless they are part of the First Project's signed price.
  5. Appiex alone decides whether to accept a project and on what price and terms. No commission is due on projects Appiex declines.

6. When and how you are paid

  1. Commission is earned only on money Appiex actually receives. For each client payment that clears, you earn 15% (or 5% for repeat work) of that payment's net amount.
  2. Appiex pays within 7 business days after each client payment clears.
  3. Payment methods: Wise, PayPal, Payoneer, bank transfer, or bKash/Nagad (Bangladesh only). Commission is calculated in USD. Payments to Bangladesh in BDT use the bank or wallet rate on the payment date.
  4. The minimum payout is USD 25. Smaller balances roll over to the next payment.
  5. Transfer fees charged by the payment provider are deducted from the payout.
  6. Before the first payout, Appiex may ask for your full legal name, a government ID and your payout account details, to prevent fraud and meet legal requirements.
  7. You are responsible for declaring and paying any taxes on your commission in your own country.

7. Refunds, cancellations and clawback

  1. If Appiex refunds any client payment, or a payment is charged back, the commission paid on that amount is deducted from your future payouts or must be repaid within 30 days.
  2. If the client cancels the project, commission is due only on payments Appiex keeps.

8. Your conduct

  1. Don't make promises on Appiex's behalf about price, timelines, features or results.
  2. Don't send spam, buy ads on the "Appiex" brand name, or create websites, pages or profiles that look like official Appiex channels.
  3. Don't offer the client part of your commission or any other incentive without Appiex's written approval.
  4. Only share a client's contact details with their permission, and in line with applicable data-protection law.
  5. You are an independent party, not an employee, agent or partner of Appiex, and you can't sign anything on Appiex's behalf.

9. Fraud and termination

  1. Appiex may reject a referral, withhold commission or remove you from the Program for fraud, self-referral, fake leads, spam, or breach of these terms.
  2. Either party may leave the Program at any time. Commission already earned on valid referrals is still paid under these terms.

10. Changes to the Program

Appiex may change these terms or end the Program with 30 days' notice on this page and by email to active Referrers. Referrals submitted before the change follow the terms in force at the time of submission.

11. Liability

Appiex's total liability to a Referrer under the Program is limited to the commission owed but unpaid. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential losses.

12. Privacy

Referral data is handled under the Appiex Privacy Policy (appiex.com/privacy).

13. Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of Bangladesh. The parties will first try to resolve a dispute in good faith within 30 days. If that fails, the courts of Dhaka have exclusive jurisdiction. Questions: contact@appiex.com.
