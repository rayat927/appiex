LEGAL FRAMEWORK & SOW GOVERNANCE

Appendix B: Partnership Program Terms & Rules

Framework terms covering White-label delivery, Co-build engagements, IP transfer, and SLAs.

1. Parties and scope

These terms apply between Appiex ("Appiex") and an agency, studio, consultancy or freelancer accepted into the Appiex Partner Program ("Partner"). They cover two models: White-label and Co-build. Introducing clients for commission is covered only by the Referral Program Terms.

2. Joining

  1. Partners apply through appiex.com/partners. Appiex may accept or decline any application.
  2. Both parties sign the Appiex Mutual NDA (Appendix C) before any client information is shared.
  3. Joining is free and doesn't oblige either party to send or accept any project.

3. White-label model

  1. Client relationship: The Partner contracts with, invoices and manages its own client ("End Client"). Appiex contracts only with the Partner.
  2. Branding: Appiex delivers under the Partner's brand and doesn't put its own name, logo or credits on deliverables, unless the Partner asks.
  3. Communication: Appiex contacts the End Client only when the Partner asks in writing, and then on the Partner's behalf.
  4. Pricing: Appiex charges the Partner its published partner rates or a fixed quote in the SOW. The Partner sets its own price to the End Client.
  5. Payment liability: The Partner pays Appiex's invoices on time whether or not the End Client has paid the Partner.

4. Co-build model

For joint products, a separate written agreement sets out each party's contribution, ownership of code and IP, revenue share, decision-making and exit terms before work starts. Without that agreement, sections 3, 5 and 6 apply as if the project were white label.

5. Payment terms

  1. Fixed-price projects: 40% before kickoff, 30% when staging is delivered, 30% at launch or handover. Hourly or retainer work: invoiced every two weeks or monthly in advance, as the SOW states.
  2. Invoices are due within 7 days. Accepted methods: Wise, Stripe, international wire, bank transfer, bKash/Nagad.
  3. If an invoice is more than 14 days overdue, Appiex may pause work until it's paid. Timelines move by the length of the pause.
  4. Prices exclude taxes and third-party costs (hosting, licences, paid APIs), which are passed through at cost or billed directly to the Partner.

6. Scope, changes and acceptance

  1. Each SOW defines the deliverables, the timeline and the number of revision rounds (2 rounds per design phase unless stated otherwise).
  2. Work outside the SOW is a change request, quoted in writing before it starts.
  3. The Partner has 7 days after each delivery to report defects against the SOW. Otherwise the deliverable is accepted.
  4. Delays caused by late content, feedback or access from the Partner or End Client move the timeline by the same amount.

7. Delivery standards and support

  1. Lighthouse Performance score of 90+ on mobile for the templates in scope at handover, excluding third-party scripts and content added by the client.
  2. 90 days of free bug fixes after launch, for defects against the SOW. New features are change requests.
  3. Response times during the support window: critical (production down) first response within 4 hours, 7 days a week; high within 1 business day; normal within 2 business days.
  4. Hosting uptime depends on the hosting provider's own SLA. Appiex sets up uptime monitoring and alerts at launch, but doesn't guarantee third-party infrastructure.
  5. Ongoing support after 90 days is available as a monthly retainer.

8. Intellectual property

  1. When each invoice for a deliverable is paid in full, all rights in that custom deliverable transfer to the Partner, who may transfer them to the End Client.
  2. Appiex keeps ownership of its pre-existing tools, libraries, templates and know-how. The Partner and End Client receive a permanent, royalty-free licence to use any of these that are built into the deliverable.
  3. Open-source components stay under their own licences.

9. Confidentiality, portfolio and non-solicitation

  1. The Mutual NDA (Appendix C) applies to all partnership work.
  2. Portfolio: Appiex won't show white-label work in its portfolio, case studies, social media or sales material without the Partner's written permission.
  3. Appiex doesn't solicit End Clients: during the partnership and for 24 months after the last project for an End Client, Appiex won't knowingly solicit or accept work directly from that End Client unless the Partner agrees in writing.
  4. Partner doesn't poach staff: during the partnership and for 12 months after, the Partner won't directly hire or engage Appiex team members who worked on its projects without Appiex's written agreement.

10. Warranties and liability

  1. Appiex will perform the work with reasonable skill and care, using properly licensed materials.
  2. The Partner is responsible for the content, data and instructions it or its End Client provides, and for its contract with the End Client.
  3. Each party's total liability for a project is limited to the fees paid or payable for that project in the 12 months before the claim. Neither party is liable for lost profits or indirect loss. These limits don't apply to breach of confidentiality, non-payment, or fraud.

11. Term and termination

  1. The partnership continues until either party ends it with 30 days' written notice.
  2. Either party may end it immediately for a material breach not fixed within 14 days of written notice.
  3. On termination, the Partner pays for all work done up to the termination date, and Appiex hands over all paid-for deliverables. Sections 8, 9 and 10 continue to apply after termination.

12. General

The parties are independent contractors. Neither may bind the other. Changes to these terms are announced with 30 days' notice and don't affect SOWs already signed. Governing law: Bangladesh. Disputes are first escalated to both founders for good-faith resolution within 30 days, then to arbitration in Dhaka under the Arbitration Act 2001, in English. If the parties agree, a project may instead be settled by a neutral arbitration body. Questions: partners@appiex.com.
