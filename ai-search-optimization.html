The short answer

What do AEO, GEO and AIO mean?

SEO improves your site's eligibility and visibility in search. AEO (answer engine optimization) emphasizes clear answers to real user questions. GEO (generative engine optimization) focuses on accurate, useful material that AI-assisted systems may retrieve or cite. AIO is used inconsistently as an industry term; we treat it here as optimization for AI search experiences, not a separate ranking system.

There is no secret AEO markup or paid way to force a ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, Gemini, or Copilot citation. The same fundamentals of discoverable, relevant, original content and reputable external references still matter.

A transparent process

What happens in an AI search audit?

  1. Discover: review Search Console, priority pages, competitors and potential audience questions.
  2. Diagnose: inspect canonical URLs, search snippets, first-screen answers, page structure and data reliability.
  3. Implement: improve selected high-intent landing pages, internal links, clarity, and verifiable business information.
  4. Measure: compare query groups and conversions over time, avoiding inflated claims from tiny datasets.

Frequently asked questions

Questions businesses ask about AEO and GEO

Can Appiex make my website appear in ChatGPT answers?

We can improve discoverability, clarity, and the quality of supporting evidence. No agency can guarantee an answer engine will mention or cite a site.

Is GEO different from SEO?

GEO emphasizes how AI-assisted search systems retrieve and reference material. In practice, Google states that strong SEO fundamentals remain the foundation of visibility in its generative search features.

Do I need FAQ schema for AI search?

No. Clear, useful FAQs can help readers, but FAQ structured data does not provide a shortcut to AI inclusion and Google has deprecated its FAQ rich result feature.

Do backlinks help AEO and GEO?

Relevant editorial links and mentions can help people discover credible resources. Buying links or mass-posting the same URL to forums creates risk and rarely produces qualified business traffic.

How should I measure AI search traffic?

Track nonbranded clicks, visibility and conversions in Search Console and analytics; monitor observable referral sources and third-party AI mentions separately. AI citation data is incomplete and should not be presented as a precise census.

How long does the process take?

The initial audit and implementation can be scoped as a project. Search indexing, algorithmic exposure and external citations have no guaranteed timetable.

Assistive Agent Optimization (AAO)

Make a business easy for an AI assistant to evaluate and contact — with your permission.

Assistive agents need accessible, consistent facts before they can shortlist a provider: what the business offers, where it delivers, how to assess fit, where to read evidence, and which actions require human approval. AAO complements search discoverability and conversion usability; it does not guarantee an AI assistant will choose or recommend any company.

Can an AI assistant hire or book Appiex automatically?

No. The public site provides information and a user-controlled enquiry flow. We do not offer an autonomous contract, payment, booking or agent action API. The team confirms availability, suitability and pricing directly.

Does AAO replace SEO, AEO and GEO?

No. Google continues to recommend standard technical SEO, visible helpful content, and factual structured data for AI-enabled search. AAO adds clarity for agent decision-making and permission-aware task completion.
