5. How to Validate Evidence Before You Buy

Ask every shortlisted provider for evidence that can be checked: a live URL or private walkthrough, the exact scope delivered, the measurement window, the data source, and permission to attribute the result. A screenshot without a date, baseline, methodology, or client context is not enough to support a performance claim.

  • Performance: compare the same URL, device class, network profile and reporting window.
  • SEO: separate branded from non-branded queries and report qualified leads, not only impressions.
  • Automation: document the previous manual process, exception rate, human approvals and operating cost.
  • Commercial results: identify other changes made during the period before attributing an outcome to one intervention.

Appiex publishes client names, quotations and numeric outcomes only when supporting evidence and publication permission are available. Otherwise, examples are explicitly presented as planning scenarios.
