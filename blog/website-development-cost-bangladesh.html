What to include in a website development quotation

Use the price ranges in this guide as planning estimates, not guaranteed market prices. To compare Bangladeshi agencies fairly, ask each supplier to quote against the same brief and include the total cost after launch.

  • Pages and templates: required languages, editable CMS fields, content migration and mobile layouts.
  • Ecommerce: catalog size, payment gateway eligibility, courier integration, stock updates and refund handling.
  • SEO and analytics: crawlable pages, unique metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap and form conversion events.
  • Ownership: domain, hosting, repository access, third-party licences, backups and handover documentation.
  • Aftercare: hosting renewals, security patches, support hours and the cost of future feature requests.

What is the difference between a cheap template website and a custom-developed website? +

Low-cost template sites often use nulled themes and 20+ heavy plugins that load in 6–10 seconds, break during updates, and struggle to convert mobile buyers. A custom-engineered site loads in under a second, passes Google Core Web Vitals, and is built around your exact sales funnel.

What are the yearly maintenance and hosting costs in Bangladesh? +

Domain registration typically costs BDT 1,200–2,500/year, and reliable cloud/VPS hosting ranges from BDT 6,000 to BDT 36,000/year depending on traffic and storage. Appiex includes 90 days of complimentary post-launch bug support with every build.
