# Appiex search opportunity: 2026 agentic AI and buyer-intent expansion

Updated 6 November 2026. This research plan contains 71 **candidate phrases**, not verified top-ranking terms.

## Scope and constraints
- The uploaded master CSV contains **600,000 keyword candidates**, many mechanically generated. A complete canonical mapping of all 600,000 rows was prepared as a downloadable compressed CSV for research. **None of those candidates is automatically published on a website page**, because publishing all would be keyword stuffing and create doorway-page risk.
- This repository file (`seo/2026-11-06-emerging-buyer-keywords.csv`) holds a smaller operational shortlist for commercial, comparative and informational content.
- No Google Search Console query export, verified Ahrefs/Google Ads keyword volumes, local rank trackers or confirmed conversion values were available. All volumes/difficulty fields remain unmeasured.
- Existing canonical pages are prioritized over proposed geo/industry duplicates. Country labels mean research targets, not established offices or ranking markets.

## Why agentic AI is being prioritized
1. McKinsey's **25 August 2026** research reports increasing agent deployments among very large organizations and continuing difficulty capturing measurable ROI. This supports industry interest, not a guaranteed conversion or rank.
   Source: https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai
2. A third-party **27 October 2026** Google Trends study (GrowAxis) compares US, UAE, Saudi, Egypt and Netherlands searches and reports higher normalized interest for 'AI agents' and 'AI automation' in specified comparisons. The study is by a commercial vendor, and Google Trends figures are **relative 0–100 indexes, not absolute search volume**. Re-check directly.
   Source: https://www.growaxis.net/insights/what-businesses-search-ai-2026
3. Google's official Trends instructions recommend checking query interest by geographic market and tying it to actual Search Console data. Creating content solely because a phrase is trending is not helpful.
   Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/trends-start
4. Google says meta-keywords tags are ignored and stuffing/doorway pages are against its policies.
   Sources: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/special-tags
   https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
